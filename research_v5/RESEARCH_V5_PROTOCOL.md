# Extended analysis protocol recorded before inspecting new results

Author Farzeen Khan. Date 24 October 2026. Earlier v3/v4 outputs are preserved.

The revised gravity baseline is the identified CSR RL06.3 all-corrections mascon record, downloaded from the provider with land mask, native mascon mapping and checksums. All analyses stop at September 2024 for TWS and September 2023 for full-balance GWS; attribution stops in 2023 with WaterGAP. Original unknown-provenance GRACE and documented COST-G/G3P processing are sensitivity records, not independent gravity observations. Special CSR nominal-month assignments are audited, without interpolating the mission gap. No smoothing or gain factor is applied to CSR contrary to provider guidance.

Objective 1: compute basin series and seasonal Sen/Mann–Kendall statistics, FDR, common-support source comparisons and full-compartment groundwater sensitivities. Spatial outputs are basin-level maps. Native mascon overlap and weight concentration describe shared spatial support; they do not estimate true degrees of freedom. All small basins retain the provider caution flag. Individual grid-point inference is not used for CSR.

Objective 2: retain meteorology-only, naturalized-parent model emulators with the prior chronological train/validation/test protocol. Add controlled Ridge feature ablations (season, current weather, lagged weather and memory), tuning within training and selecting by validation. Compare original parent forcing with an independently sourced CHIRPS precipitation forcing as a covariate-shift sensitivity, not a new causal counterfactual. Assess observed climate response using training-selected precipitation accumulation/lag pairs and an untouched later period; do not call these causally identified response times.

Attribution: compute O, C, A, H=A−C, E=O−A and R=O−C on common support, retaining R=H+E. Fit monthly-fixed-effect OLS trends with the same dates for all terms. Use 999 shared-multiplier calendar-block wild-bootstrap replicates at block lengths 1, 2 and 3 years, retaining original missingness. Report trend intervals and a discrepancy-magnitude diagnostic D=abs(beta_E)/(abs(beta_H)+abs(beta_E)). D is not a human fraction or an error-budget allocation. Compare source and soil-structure alternatives; do not assign the unexplained remainder to pumping.

Groundwater: retain raw provider flags plus exclusion and fixed-eligibility sensitivities. Resample coordinate station groups rather than treating colocated wells as independent; compare common-year raw, climate-controlled, detrended and contiguous-change associations. Annual records remain annual. Report well-level trend direction and network support. No head-to-storage coefficient is invented. Pressure proxies remain descriptive and cannot validate measured pumping quantities.

Research contribution is proposed as an integrated attribution-credibility assessment connecting product dependence, parent-model emulation, discrepancy and observation-network uncertainty. No claim of first-ever novelty, Q1 acceptance or causal human depletion is prespecified. Numerical results, not desired signs or high correlations, determine the conclusions.

Exploratory reservoir amendment, 25 October 2026, requested after the groundwater analyses: acquire MSEA-Res v2, assign reservoir outlet coordinates to Thai basin polygons, retain a fixed pre-2004 reservoir population, require all selected members in a retained month, and compare screened scene estimates separately from provider-interpolated records. Test raw and matched-support month/trend-adjusted relationships, with calendar-year cluster resampling. Do not retune the climate model. These results test surface-water component consistency, not an observed human-only counterfactual. The analysis was not prespecified before the study began.

Exploratory network amendment: the initial fixed-eligibility comparison retained variable annual availability and changed the early-record station mix. Add an all-well/common-period comparison and a strict ten-of-ten-year panel restricted to 2013–2022. Compare them on the same observed storage years, with mission-gap years still missing. This separates a period effect from a network-composition effect; it is an exploratory extension rather than a retrospectively preregistered test.
