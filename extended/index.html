Groundwater evidence

Heads remain head measurements. No storage coefficient is invented. Network panels and common years change the comparison; a correlation is not a calibrated human-storage validation.

Independent reservoir evidence

MSEA-Res scene estimates retain gaps; Level 2 is provider-interpolated. A fixed pre-2004 population is used. Adjusted correlations remove calendar-month effects and linear trend on common months. Reservoirs reflect climate and operations; they do not directly validate groundwater storage.

Spatial interpretation

All 22 principal basins are below CSR’s approximate 200,000 km² caution threshold. Shared native mascons mean neighbouring basin estimates are statistically dependent. Use basin results for screening and monitoring priorities.
