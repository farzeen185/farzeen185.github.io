WHAT THE OBSERVATIONS SAY

Groundwater and reservoir evidence

Wells are annual, often with only eight paired storage years. Reservoirs represent surface water, not groundwater. Station bootstrap intervals are conditional on observed years; correlations do not identify human causation.
